How to get transcripts from Invoca

1
Log into Invoca → click Conversation Review in the left nav.
2
Filter by franchise using the Campaign or Tracking Number filter. Set your date range.
3
Click on any call → scroll down to see the full transcript. Select all the text, copy it.
4
Paste it below. You can paste multiple transcripts at once — just separate them with a blank line or "---". Aim for 5–20 calls per analysis for best results.
5
Hit Analyze calls and Claude will extract customer language, objections, job types, and ad copy ideas.
