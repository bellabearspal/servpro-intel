Connect your dashboard to a Google Sheet for persistent cloud storage

Step 1 — Paste your Apps Script URL

Once you've deployed your Google Apps Script (see the setup guide), paste the Web App URL below. This is the bridge between your dashboard and your Google Sheet.

Step 2 — Import data (it saves automatically)

When you import any CSV file using the Import Data wizard, it automatically saves to your Google Sheet. On every future visit, your data loads from the Sheet instantly — no re-importing needed.

Saved datasets

Connect to Google Sheets to see saved datasets.

Quick Setup Guide

Follow these steps once to connect your Google Sheet:

  1. 1Go to sheets.google.com → create a new sheet → name it SERVPRO Intel.
  2. 2In the sheet, click Extensions → Apps Script. A new tab opens.
  3. 3Delete all the default code. Paste in the entire contents of your Code.gs file (download below).
  4. 4Click Deploy → New deployment. Type: Web app. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone. Click Deploy.
  5. 5Copy the Web app URL that appears and paste it above in Step 1.
  6. 6That's it — your dashboard now saves all imported data to the cloud.
